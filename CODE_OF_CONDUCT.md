# Code of Conduct

Atoll follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be welcoming, kind and patient, whatever someone's background or level of experience.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing someone's private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer privately, using the email on [@donjordano's GitHub profile](https://github.com/donjordano). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments or commits, or ban someone from the project, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
